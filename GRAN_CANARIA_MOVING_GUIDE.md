## Why Gran Canaria?

Gran Canaria, part of Spain's Canary Islands, is often called a "mini continent" due to its diverse landscapes and microclimates. Key advantages for Polish winter escapees:

- **Perfect winter weather**: 20-25°C in winter vs -5°C to 5°C in Poland
- **Lower cost of living** than mainland Spain (Madrid, Barcelona)
- **Tax benefits**: Lower VAT equivalent (IGIC at 7% vs 21% in mainland Spain)
- **EU territory**: No visa required for Polish citizens
- **Thriving expat & digital nomad community**
- **~300 sunny days per year**
- **Lower energy costs** (no heating required)

### Climate Comparison (Winter)

| Month | Gran Canaria | Poland (Warsaw) |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| December | 18-22°C | -2 to 3°C |
| January | 17-21°C | -4 to 1°C |
| February | 17-21°C | -3 to 2°C |
| March | 18-22°C | 1 to 8°C |

**Note**: The "Calima" phenomenon (Saharan dust wind) occurs periodically in winter, reducing air quality temporarily.

## Legal Requirements for Polish Citizens

### EU Citizen Rights

As a Polish citizen, you have the **right to live and work in Spain** without a visa. However:

- Stays **< 3 months**: No registration needed, just valid passport/ID
- Stays **> 3 months**: Must register and obtain documents

### Required Documents

#### 1. NIE Number (Número de Identidad de Extranjero)
Your foreigner identification number, essential for:
- Opening bank account
- Signing rental contract
- Working/invoicing
- Buying property
- Getting a phone contract

**How to get it:**
1. Book appointment at Oficina de Extranjería (foreigners office)
2. Fill out **EX18 form**
3. Pay fee: **€12** (Modelo 790, código 012)
4. Bring: Passport, proof of address, reason for stay

#### 2. EU Registration Certificate ("Green NIE")
Required if staying > 3 months. Proves EU residency rights.

**Requirements:**
- Valid passport/ID
- Proof of one of:
  - Employment in Spain
  - Self-employment registration
  - Sufficient financial means (€7,200/year minimum for single person)
  - Private health insurance (if not working locally)

**Documents needed:**
- EX18 form
- Proof of address (rental contract)
- Proof of income/savings (bank statements)
- Health insurance certificate
- Passport/ID photos

#### 3. Empadronamiento (Municipal Registration)
Registration at your local town hall (Ayuntamiento). Required for:
- Accessing public services
- Free public transport card
- Healthcare card (Tarjeta Sanitaria)
- Utility contracts in your name

**To get it:**
- Visit local Oficina de Atención al Ciudadano
- Bring: Passport/ID + proof of address (rental contract/utility bill)
- Free of charge
- Can be done without NIE

### Step-by-Step Registration Process

1. **Week 1-2**: Arrive, find temporary accommodation
2. **Week 2-3**: Find long-term apartment, sign contract
3. **Week 3-4**: Get Empadronamiento with rental contract
4. **Week 4-6**: Apply for NIE/Green Card
5. **Week 6-8**: Open bank account, complete setup

### Health Insurance Options

As EU citizen, you can:
1. Use **EHIC card** (temporary coverage)
2. Apply for **Tarjeta Sanitaria** (Spanish health card) - free with employment
3. Get **private insurance**: €50-150/month (required for non-working residents)

Popular private insurers: Sanitas, Adeslas, DKV, Mapfre

### Winter Weather by Area

- **South (Puerto Rico, Mogán)**: Most sheltered, warmest, least wind
- **Southwest (Maspalomas)**: Sunny but can be windy
- **East (Telde, Vecindario)**: Moderate, occasional clouds
- **North (Las Palmas)**: More clouds, rain possible, cooler
- **Mountains (interior)**: Cooler, can be rainy

**Recommendation for winter**: Southern coast (Puerto Rico, Mogán) for guaranteed sun, or Las Palmas for social life + occasional rainy days.

## Practical Tips

### Before You Leave Poland

1. **Documents to prepare:**
   - Valid passport or ID card (check expiry!)
   - European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) - order via NFZ
   - Driving license (Polish is valid in Spain)
   - Copies of important documents (store in cloud)

2. **Financial preparation:**
   - Notify your Polish bank of travel
   - Consider **Revolut/Wise** for fee-free spending in euros
   - Have 2-3 months expenses saved
   - Research opening Spanish bank account (BBVA, Santander, N26)

3. **Health:**
   - Stock up on any prescription medications
   - Get EHIC card
   - Consider travel/health insurance for first months

### On Arrival

1. **First days:**
   - Get local SIM card (Movistar, Vodafone, Orange, or MVNOs like Lowi)
   - Download **GuaGua Global** app for bus schedules
   - Download **Google Maps** offline for Gran Canaria
   - Join Facebook expat groups

2. **Setting up:**
   - Open bank account (once you have NIE)
   - Register for Empadronamiento
   - Apply for NIE/Green Card
   - Get a Spanish phone number

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Shopping:**
   - Shop at Mercadona, Hiperdino, Lidl for groceries
   - Visit local markets for fresh produce (cheaper than supermarkets)
   - Use "Too Good To Go" app for discounted food

2. **Transport:**
   - Get resident transport card for **free bus travel**
   - Walking and cycling are practical in many areas
   - Car sharing (Cicar, TopCar) for occasional trips

3. **Entertainment:**
   - Many free beaches and hiking trails
   - Public events and fiestas are often free
   - Happy hour deals at bars (usually 5-8 PM)

4. **Accommodation:**
   - Negotiate rent (especially for 6+ month contracts)
   - Look for utilities included in rent
   - Consider flatshares to save 40-50%

### What to Pack

**Bring from Poland:**
- Electronics (same quality, often cheaper in Poland)
- Winter clothes for Polish visits
- Specialty Polish foods you'll miss
- Important documents

**Buy in Gran Canaria:**
- Light clothing (cheaper locally)
- Beach gear
- Sunscreen (though can be expensive - bring from Poland)

### Cultural Tips

- **Siesta culture**: Many shops close 2-5 PM
- **Late dining**: Dinner often 9-10 PM
- **Relaxed pace**: "Mañana" attitude - be patient
- **Spanish language**: English widely spoken in tourist areas, but Spanish helps
- **Tipping**: Not expected, but appreciated (round up or 5-10%)
